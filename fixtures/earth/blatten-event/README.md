# Blatten · 30 May 2025 surface

**Elevation.** Simeon Schmauß - created from BAFU/swisstopo Rapid Mapping oblique imagery, CC BY 4.0. Hallmark cropped the author's 1 m v2 elevation raster from the 30 May flight. This is the author's photogrammetric reconstruction; the federal catalogue also lists its own DSM for this date. [Original numeric raster](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1VLut72e4zpnU5xSD8tnQBTNI021GGKkl/view) · [Creator's processing notes, attribution and download list](https://docs.google.com/document/d/12vx7Han8xQMpate9QUdXkMHgcBfkpXxJn0F6ctbs79Y/edit) · [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)

**Imagery.** Source: FOEN, swisstopo. The texture and overview are crops of the federal RGB orthophoto from the same flight. Hallmark resampled the RGB imagery for display. The locator photograph spans 1,000 × 700 m in LV95 and outlines the analysis grid. [Federal survey inventory](https://www.swisstopo.admin.ch/en/rapidmapping-news-en-20261021) · [Rapid Mapping terms, including prescribed attribution](https://www.swisstopo.admin.ch/en/service-rapid-mapping)

**Processing.** The manifest records the exact source SHA-256, affine transform, window, source NoData and output checksums. Read band 1 at column 2582, row 3290, width 400, height 400, in zero-based source pixels. Save row-major little-endian float32 at native spacing with source NoData mapped to NaN. All 160,000 values in this window are finite. RGB uses the official EPSG:2056 orthophoto at the same projected bounds, area-averaged to the native DSM cells. The independently dated baseline is documented in [the swissALTI3D fixture](../blatten/README.md).

**Interpretation.** Vegetation and structures remain in the reconstruction. The author reports 1.6 m mean positional error over 18 manually placed controls. This is a survey-wide diagnostic. The route, endpoints and local approval workflow are illustrative Hallmark decisions. The crop includes no per-cell confidence layer, strength measurement, underground water observations or operational access boundary.
